# Launch Acceptance Design

`docs/WORK.md` is live status and decision record. `docs/OPERATIONS.md` owns
procedures; `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` owns topology/release/rollback. Evidence may be
linked from release records but must stay sanitized.

Allowed statuses: `not_started`, `in_progress`, `passed`, `passed_with_notes`,
`blocked`, `waived`, `not_applicable`.
